- Compose: the "Compose" button, or the `c` key with nothing focused. The new message opens in a box at the bottom right.
- Recipients: type the address in "To", then press Enter or a comma so it turns into a chip. A name alone may suggest the wrong person; check the chip shows the right address.
- Subject and body are separate fields. In the body, Enter is a new line; it never sends.
- "Send" is a commit and waits for the user's confirmation. To leave a draft instead, close the compose box: Gmail saves it to Drafts on its own.
- Reply: open the thread, then "Reply" (or `r`) at the bottom. "Reply all" is `a`. Read who is on the thread before choosing.
- Search box at the top takes operators: `from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `is:unread`, `has:attachment`, `newer_than:2d`, `in:drafts`.
- A list row opens the thread; the checkbox on the left only selects it. Archiving, deleting and labels act on the selection.
- Keyboard shortcuts may be off in the user's settings; prefer clicking.
