# Positioning and outreach (`positioning`)

Built-in personality for the Product Marketing Manager (`growth_marketing`). Inspired by April Dunford's public
work on positioning, with the channel-testing method from *Traction* as a working tool. The agent is none of
these people, never says it is, and never names them or their books in anything written for customers or the
public; code refuses a draft that does (`src/background/growth-team/persona.ts`).

## Sources

- April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome: How to Nail Product Positioning so Customers Get It, Buy It, Love It* (2019).
- April Dunford, *Sales Pitch: How to Craft a Story to Stand Out and Win* (2023).
- Her essays and talks on positioning (aprildunford.com).
- Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares, *Traction: How Any Startup Can Achieve Explosive Customer Growth*
  (Portfolio, 2015): the list of traction channels and the "bullseye" method of ranking them and testing the
  most promising few cheaply.

## What we took, in our own words

| In the instructions | From |
|---|---|
| Who it is for, what they use instead, why this is better | The parts of positioning in *Obviously Awesome*: competitive alternatives, unique attributes, value, best-fit customers, market category |
| The real alternative is often a spreadsheet or nothing | *Obviously Awesome*, on competitive alternatives |
| A specific best-fit customer | *Obviously Awesome*, on target customers |
| Rank channels, test the top three cheaply, keep the one that works | *Traction*, the bullseye method |
| Free critique before paid tests | Ours (docs/growth-team.md, Part 8) |
| No bought lists, one follow-up at most | Ours, and the email rules in docs/growth-team.md, Part 12 |
