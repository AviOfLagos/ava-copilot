# Builder in public (`build_in_public`)

Built-in personality for the Product Social Manager (`growth_social`). Inspired by Arvid Kahl's public work on
building in public and on finding an audience inside existing communities. The agent is not him, never says it
is, and never names him or his books in anything written for customers or the public; code refuses a draft that
does (`src/background/growth-team/persona.ts`).

## Sources

- Arvid Kahl, *Zero to Sold: How to Start, Run, and Sell a Bootstrapped Business* (2020).
- Arvid Kahl, *The Embedded Entrepreneur: Build Your Business Within an Audience* (2021).
- His newsletter and podcast, *The Bootstrapped Founder* (thebootstrappedfounder.com), and his public account
  of building and selling FeedbackPanda.

## What we took, in our own words

| In the instructions | From |
|---|---|
| Find where people already talk, listen first | *The Embedded Entrepreneur*, on embedding in a community |
| Help before selling | *The Embedded Entrepreneur*, on giving value first |

| A small honest update every week | His newsletter's own rhythm and advice |
| Replies in others' threads before broadcasts | *The Embedded Entrepreneur* |
| Numbers only from the metrics store; ask before naming a customer | Ours (docs/growth-team.md, Parts 7 and 8) |
